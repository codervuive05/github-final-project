# Code of Conduct — github-final-project

File URL: https://github.com/codervuive05/github-final-project/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

## Our Pledge
We pledge a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Show empathy, respect differing opinions, accept constructive feedback, take responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and prioritize community wellbeing. Sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, political or personal attacks, harassment, disclosure of private information without permission, and other inappropriate conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards, investigate violations fairly, and take proportionate corrective action. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, documentation, or other contributions that violate this policy and explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope
This policy applies in all community spaces and when someone officially represents the community in public, including official email, social media accounts, and appointed representatives at events.

## Enforcement
Report abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior to community leaders at code-of-conduct@example.com. Include the people involved, a description of the incident, and relevant links or evidence. Leaders acknowledge reports, review and investigate promptly and fairly, protect the reporter's privacy and security, assess the facts, apply the guidelines below, and communicate the outcome when appropriate.

## Enforcement Guidelines
1. Correction: inappropriate language or unprofessional behavior receives a private written warning explaining the violation; a public apology may be requested.
2. Warning: a single incident or repeated violations receive a warning with consequences. No contact with the people involved, including unsolicited contact with moderators, is allowed for a specified time, within community spaces or external channels. Violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. Temporary Ban: serious or sustained misconduct results in exclusion from community interaction for a specified period. No public or private contact with the people involved or moderators is permitted; violations may lead to a permanent ban.
4. Permanent Ban: sustained violations, harassment, aggression, or disparagement of individuals or classes of people results in permanent exclusion from all community interaction.
